# Known limitations

- iPhone and iOS 17 or newer are required. Android, iPad, and Mac Catalyst are unsupported.
- Backup JSON contains journal records and photo filenames, but not the photo bytes. CSV exports omit photos.
- Restore replaces the local journal rather than merging conflicting records.
- Local notifications depend on user permission and iOS delivery policy.
- The app records observations and does not diagnose plants or predict propagation success.
